## Surf and beach

### Riding the wave — from stage 2

Robinson surfs by himself, at the tempo of the stage. The NOTAX stamp fires by itself at the nearest monster; every hit splashes ink onto the ones around it.

**Green hearts** are 💚 — every heart is one (a soft *blubb*). Every kill drops a heart that lands on the wave. Hearts you fly past drift away behind you — gone. A **giant heart** (from stage 5, high on Backflip arcs) is worth 25. The red **magnet** (Heart Magnet) pulls hearts to you for 20 s.

A **gold diamond** gives +1 stamina for this run. A loss costs nothing but the run: you wake with half your stamina. Lost the first fight? Open the Ledger — Faster Stamping or Wider Splash.

### Jump — tap D — from stage 2

**Tap D** (or the right half) as you come up to a mussel — the red **D** sign glows: a **big jump**. The mussel dies and you lose nothing. In the air the **underside of your board is a weapon**: whatever it touches dies and you bounce up off it. Your body is not — fly into a monster with it and it hurts.

No tap: a small hop — the mussel still dies, but it costs **1 stamina**. A tap with no mussel near: a little hop, nothing more.

### Boost and combos — from stage 3

Every jump off a mussel is a **combo**: you get a **boost** — faster, for a fixed stretch. Jump again before it runs out and it starts over. Flawless = flow the whole way.

Any hit ends the boost and the combo.

### Nosegrab and Backflip — from stage 4

How you fly depends on your tempo and on the wave where you take off. **Nosegrab** (from stage 4): with boost, or on a steep spot — higher and further. **Backflip** (from stage 5): boost *and* a steep spot.

From a Nosegrab on, **the next mussel surfaces exactly where you land**. Tap D again as you come down on it: the chain goes on — big jumps keep you high on the steep part of the face, so Backflips come in chains.

In a **Nosegrab** the nose of the board is a weapon too; in a **Backflip** all of you is.

### Wall Leap — tap A — from stage 8

A green **wall of monsters** swims at you, faster than the water — and the wave throws your followers (the black wake lice) ahead. A green **A** sign swims at you ahead of the wall: **tap A** (or the left half) **the moment you pass it** — it says TAP, and the wall is just coming into view:

the boomerang flies **back** · you climb · you **leap over the wall** and dive where the thrown ones came down. The wall passes under you; in the air you cannot be hurt and every touch is a kill.

A small **herald** swims just behind the sign — one stamp takes it. The window opens the moment the sign touches you and lasts at least a third of a second; the faster the stage, the earlier the sign comes.

The whole leap is the combo: **nothing can hurt you, all of you is a weapon**, and it goes as high as the tower is tall. In a boss fight a good A lands you **right on top of him** (BOSS SLAM).

Too early: you land in the wall (the cat asks “mrrp?”). Too late: it hits you (“MAU!”).

### VOID — the life-saver — from stage 6

VOID drinks the **hearts** you collect (the ring next to your hearts). When your stamina would hit 0, it fires by itself: **30 % stamina back**, root lightning from kill to kill, and a flood that washes your followers away. It also pulls you out of a crevasse. It charges again from the next 40 hearts (Quick Charge: fewer).

### The First Mate — from stage 10

He rides ahead of you and throws pages. Every third attack he winds up (a red ring and "!") and slams a **shockwave** up the face: hop over it (D) or dive under it with the Wall Leap (A). If it misses, he glows — he is **open** and takes double damage. A good A leap lands right on him.

### Glider — hold D — from stage 11

**Hold D** (or the right half) in the air: a palm-leaf canopy opens and you **glide** — up to 2 s (Long Glide: longer). The cat glides with you on her board.

It works on the beach too, once you have it.

### Crevasses in the scree — from stage 11

In the nest and in his den the wave runs over rotten **scree**, with **crevasses** (amber sign: GAP). Early ones: a free jump (D) clears them. Wider ones: jump from the mussel in front. The widest: only the **glider** gets you across.

Falling in costs **all your stamina** — only a charged VOID saves you. Monsters keep clear of the crevasses.

### Flamethrower — hold A — from stage 11

Against the Board's **army**: keep **A held** through the boomerang, the climb and the whole leap — the flame burns into the wall in front of you, and from the air down onto it, until you dive into the water. What it catches burns and chars. Hotter Flames burns harder. Armies bring more, bigger and tougher walls.

It works on the beach walls too, once you have it.

### Poison clouds — from stage 20

From stage 20 a black **cloud** stops over you and flashes **A!**. Then it rains poison on the stretch where you ride — a little behind you and a good way ahead.

**Tap A** while it flashes: you climb back out of the rain and leap far ahead; while it still rains you are in the leap or the dive. In the leap and the dive nothing hurts you — the white-turquoise **shield** round you shows it. Tap much too early and you come down in the rain again; too late and the first drops get you. A D hop keeps you in the rain.

### The barrel — from stage 27

From stage 27 the wave **stands up** once a stage: the curl swells, falls back, swells again — and then throws its lip over you. Under the curtain: **PULL IN — HOLD A**. A quick tap is only a leap; miss it and the lip lands on you (stamina).

Inside the **barrel** the wave spins round you and monsters, mussels and hearts spiral out of the light at the end of the tube. **A**: boomerang, climb, a short leap and dive. **D**: a jump — off a mussel a big one. The stamp works as always.

At the end the tube opens top right. As soon as you see the sky, **hold A**: you ride up the wall to the **wake** top left. Let go there — **SPIT!**, the barrel throws you high into the sky. Let go too early and the wave just backs off; hold too long and you hit the wake (stamina). Tap A before the sky shows and you miss the way out.

### The cat — from stage 1

She rides on your head and warns you: **ears flat** — danger ahead; **green eyes** — prey; **looking back** — wasps from behind. With **Fishing Dive** she leaps after a rot minnow once a fight (a sure fish tail 🐟); with **Her Own Board** she surfs in your wake and dives again and again.

### Hours — your time — from stage 1

Every low tide gives you a few **hours** of low water (the cyan waves round the End-ebb compass, bottom right). Each action on the beach costs one: a coconut, a sip from the pool, a crab, a visit to the palm forest. You won't manage everything — **choose**.

When they are spent, nothing happens until you tap **Wait for the tide ⏭** (later: **End ebb**). You start with **3**; finding out more about the island earns more — the stamp, the palm forest, Crusoe's first note, his bark map, and the island itself: up to **8**.

### The beach tower — from stage 1

The right wheel on the End-ebb compass: **tap once — Silver**: at the start of every ebb the tower does the beach chores — catching crabs, drinking coconuts, rum for the tortoise — and pays for each with one of your beach hours (never a spring hour). **Tap again — Gold** (Tower II): it also rides the stage for you — up to two stages past the last one you rode yourself — at about 70 % of your usual haul — but it can lose: the odds come from your upgrades against the stage, and your stamina. **Tap again — off.** Bosses are always yours.

## Island

### Hours

**8 hours 🌊 every ebb** for the island: walking, protecting, cleansing, planting all cost hours. What you don't spend is gone.

*The sea doesn't do savings accounts. Watch the coast: the fewer hours left, the closer the waves.*

### Spring hour

**+1 ✨ every tide, for good** — earned by real deeds: the banyan, roots around it, a nest burned, the gecko, the beach tower, an offshoot, an offensive survived. Shared with the beach, always spent last.

*The golden kind. You can't harvest it. You have to earn it.*

### Rot & arms

**Rot 1–3.** Rot tiles give no sap and no harvest; from rot 2 nothing can be planted. Rot spreads only next to rot: the strongest rot neighbour is the main arm, every other one adds +1.

On the map the rot builds: **rot 1** surveyors, **rot 2** sheds and a foundation, **rot 3 a trebuchet**.

*The yacht leaks into everything — and it brings its own engineers.*

### Nests & merge

A **nest** can't be struck. Every flood it feeds **+1 rot** to the weakest tiles it owns. When two rot networks touch they **merge**: each nest also feeds a third of the other's tiles. New nests only come with the story.

*Synergies — theirs, not ours.*

### Bad Omen

**Bad Omen:** each flood the rot picks one kind of land (top centre); every network sends one spearhead (black tendril) into the nearest tile of that kind — even under the clouds. Every ten tides the Board lands supplies: one more spearhead per network.

**Trebuchet:** a rot-3 tile fires at its own root first, −1 every flood. Without a root, at the front it builds a trebuchet: it **loads one flood and fires the next**, at the neighbour nearest the banyan. Behind the front the rot builds a **tower** (3 floods) with a trebuchet on top: **range 2**. The ball always lands — a root there loses a step; a withered root stays withered for a flood, and a tile without a root gets +1 rot. Nothing is built on the steep flanks of Fire Mountain. Cleanse below 3 and it falls apart.

### Defence

**Defence = root strength + healthy rooted neighbours (max 2) + protected + a friend + you**, standing there. Odds that the tile holds: even 50 %, one ahead 80 %, two 92 %, three+ 99 %.

If it falls: +1 rot and its root −1. A root at 0 **withers** — it grows back by itself once the tile is clean, if a rooted tile is next door. New roots start at strength 2. Defence two above the attack pushes the rot back.

*Hold a line, not a dot.*

### Sap

**The banyan grows only in a flood in which at least one strike was held** (otherwise its growth sap goes into work).

The banyan works as many **healthy rooted tiles** as its level: banyan 3 sap · grove & jungle 2 · beach & slope 1 · food +1. The sap splits into **growth** (levels) and **production** (roots, towers, offshoots, cleaning).

**Harvest:** every healthy rooted food tile brings in +1 🍞 or 🥥 per flood, +1 more if protected. Robinson doesn't pick — the roots do.

**Rot 3 on a tree** burns levels to push the rot back (and the fire hits nests within two steps). At level 1 the old banyan dies — and the island with it.

### The First Mate

Bosses don't die, darling. They get **demoted**. Beat one on the beach and he crawls back into one of **his nests** — the ink-dark ones — and sulks for **three tides**. Then he walks in with a normal wave: thinner, smaller, still both attacks.

Scrub the nest he sits in and he has to move: one day of sulking lost. Scrub his **last** nest and he is gone for good. No fight needed. Your briefcase reads his mail; listen to it.

### Blight

**Blight** rises by the rot level of every rot tile you step on, +1 per scrub, +2 if you end the ebb on rot. It falls when you finish a job (rot to 0: −2, a nest gone: −4) and every flood by half the defence of your tile.

From 5 every step costs +1 hour, from 8 +2. At 10 you collapse and lose an ebb.

*Spores, ink, paperwork — it gets into the lungs.*

### Root reach

Roots are only as strong as their line to the nearest tree. **Tree tile and 1 step:** up to 3 · **2 steps:** up to 2 · **3 steps:** up to 1 · **4+:** nothing grows. **Deep Roots** pushes all of it one step further.

*Far places need a new tree. That is what offshoots are for.*

### Holding a nest

**Burn** a nest down to rot 0 (2 hours + 3 🥃 a go, −2 rot — Cleanse does nothing on a nest) and it lies **exposed** — open, but not dead. Leave it and it grows back.
To finish it, **hold it**: end the ebb on the nest. At flood the nest fights back — a fight **instead of** the beach fight. If its boss is at home, he fights too.

Win: the nest is gone for good (Blight −4). Lose: it gets +1 rot back, you collapse and lose an ebb.

### Armies

During a Board offensive, **armies** crawl out of the nests (the first offensive: exactly three, strength 5, 6 and 8 — the last one, from the far end, led by the Repo Crab). Each has a **strength**.

**March.** Every flood they walk towards the banyan — 2 steps on rot, 1 elsewhere. The red arrow shows next flood's way. Roots don't stop them. A field with an army is visible while it stands there.

**Targets.** The beach tower, offshoots, the banyan — and you. Against a building they fight like rot: **strength vs the tile's defence**, same odds. If it falls, the building loses a level (the tower falls, an offshoot at level 1 dies, the banyan never drops below 1). If it holds by 2 or more, the army loses 1 strength; a narrow hold just stops it for a flood.

**You.** An army that reaches your tile fights you first (instead of the beach fight). Win: it is gone. Lose: you collapse. You can also **attack** an army on or next to your tile: 2 hours per fight, as often as your hours last. On a tree you get help: the gecko and a small thorn tower.

### Kinship

**Kinship**: how much of the island is in you. Kind deeds raise it. The **total** sets your stage — at 15, 30, 45 and 60 — with further sight, a stronger hand, faster growth. Every point is also **free Kinship** to teach the banyan; spending doesn't lower your stage.
